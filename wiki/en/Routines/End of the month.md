# End of the month

- Money, “Received”: the total is what came in over the last 90 days; the list shows each payment and its receipt for your accountant.
- Money, “Memberships”: check “Waiting for payment” is empty or chased.
- Check the month's receipts and invoices are saved in Wiki → Documents, then tap “Download list for the accountant”.
