# Once a week

- Leads: clear “In touch”. Anyone you couldn't reach, mark “No answer” or try again.
- Members: look at “Gone quiet” and message anyone you'd like back.
- Marketing: see where new people came from this month.
- Classes: look at the next 7 days for classes that are full or nearly empty.
