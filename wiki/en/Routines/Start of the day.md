# Start of the day

- Open Today. Read “Needs you” from top to bottom.
- Answer new leads first: WhatsApp or call, then tap what happened.
- Check today's classes: who is coming, and whether anyone is marked “Not paid yet”.
- If a plan ends in the next 7 days, send that member a short message.
