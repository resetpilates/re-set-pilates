# What is this page?

This is your studio in one place. Everything people do on the website lands here: new leads, bookings, purchases and questions.

It reads from Airtable, where the records are kept. You don't need to check email or open Airtable for the daily work.

- Today: what is waiting for you and who is coming.
- Leads: people who asked about the studio.
- Classes: the next 7 days and who is booked.
- Members: everyone, with their plan. Tap a name for her full history.
- Money: payments, receipts and memberships.
- Marketing: where people come from and what the ads cost.
