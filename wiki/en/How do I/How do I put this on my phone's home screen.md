# How do I put this on my phone's home screen?

- iPhone: open this page in Safari, tap the Share button, then “Add to Home Screen”.
- Android: open it in Chrome, tap the three dots, then “Add to Home screen”.

After that it opens like an app. The passcode is asked once per phone.
