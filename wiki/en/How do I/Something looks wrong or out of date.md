# Something looks wrong or out of date

- Press Refresh at the top. The page reads Airtable again.
- Still wrong? Open the record in Airtable (“Open in Airtable”) and check it there.
- If the page asks for the passcode again, type it. That happens once a month.

For anything else, send Eli a screenshot.
