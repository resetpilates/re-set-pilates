# A new person wrote to us. What do I do?

- Open Leads. New people are under “To answer”.
- Tap WhatsApp or Call to reach them.
- Then tap what happened: “I answered”, “Booked a class”, “No answer”…

They move out of “To answer” so the list only shows who is still waiting. Try to answer the same day.
