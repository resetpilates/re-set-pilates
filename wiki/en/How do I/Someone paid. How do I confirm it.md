# Someone paid. How do I confirm it?

A purchase on the website is saved as “Pending” until you confirm the money arrived.

- Open Money. The order is under “To confirm”. Tap “Open in Airtable”.
- Set Status to “Paid”.
- Open the plan linked to it (Memberships) and set Status to “Active”.

The customer's app then stops showing “Payment pending” by itself, and their classes start counting.
