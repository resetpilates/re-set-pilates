# Where do I find a receipt?

- Open Money and find the payment, or open the customer under Members.
- Tap “Receipt”. It opens as a page you can print or send on WhatsApp.

The receipt shows the payment as it is now: “waiting” until you confirm it, “paid” after. A payment you typed into Airtable by hand has no receipt number, so it has no receipt.
