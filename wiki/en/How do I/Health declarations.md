# Health declarations

Every member fills in a health declaration and accepts the studio terms once a year, before their first booking or payment. The app asks them by itself.

The answers are private and are not shown on this page. To read them, open the “Health & Consent” tab in Airtable.

If someone needs a doctor's approval, tick “Doctor approval on file” there once you have it.
