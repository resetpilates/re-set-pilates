# How do I see everything about one customer?

- Open Members and tap her name. You can also tap a name in a class or in Money.

You see her details, her memberships, every payment with its receipt, and a full history: each class she booked, came to or missed, each purchase, and what she wrote to you.

Health answers are not shown here. The page only says whether a health declaration is on file.
