# How do I save a receipt or a document?

- Open Wiki. Documents is the first section.
- Tap “Add a document”, then “Take a photo” (for a paper receipt) or “Choose a file” (for a PDF you were sent).
- Give it a name, tap what it is (expense, invoice, tax, health…) and check the date.
- For an expense or invoice, type the amount and who was paid: they add up in “Expenses” and go into the list for the accountant.
- Tap Save. To find it later, type any word from its name, note or who was paid in the search box.
