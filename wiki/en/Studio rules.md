# Studio rules

These are the studio terms customers accept. To change one, tell Eli: it changes on the website too.

## 1. Booking classes

- Classes are booked in advance through the re:set pilates app, subject to availability.
- Reformer classes are limited to 5 participants.
- Before your first class you must accept these terms and complete the digital health declaration in the app.

## 2. Cancelling a group class

- A class can be cancelled free of charge up to 24 hours before it starts.
- A cancellation less than 24 hours before class, or a no-show, counts as a used class.
- This policy applies to memberships and class passes alike, subject to applicable law.

## 3. Late arrival

- Please arrive a few minutes before class starts.
- Late entry is at the instructor's discretion and subject to safety considerations.
- Arriving late does not extend the class.

## 4. Class passes

- Passes are bought once and do not renew automatically.
- A pass is valid for two months from the date of purchase.
- A pass is personal and cannot be transferred without the studio's approval.
- Classes not used by the end of the validity period expire, subject to applicable law.
- Exceptional requests to extend a pass are reviewed by the studio management.

## 5. Monthly memberships

- A membership is a monthly plan. Auto-renewal is your choice: you can switch it on when you buy and switch it on or off at any time in the app.
- With auto-renewal on, you are charged once a month according to the plan you chose and the consent you gave.
- The membership keeps renewing until auto-renewal is switched off or the membership is cancelled.
- No new approval is needed each month.
- Unused classes do not carry over to the next month, subject to applicable law.
- You can ask to cancel your membership at any time. Billing stops and the balance is settled in accordance with the law.

## 6. Private and duo sessions

- Sessions are arranged in advance, subject to availability.
- A package of private or duo sessions is valid for 4 months from the date of purchase.
- A session can be cancelled free of charge up to 24 hours before it starts.
- A late cancellation or a no-show counts as a used session, subject to applicable law.

## 7. Closed groups

- A Pilates class can be booked for a closed group of up to 5 participants.
- The class lasts 50 minutes.
- The studio is reserved for the group alone during the class.
- Booking is arranged in advance, subject to availability.
- A booking can be cancelled free of charge up to 24 hours before it starts. A later cancellation is charged in full, subject to applicable law.

## 8. Private events

- A private event lasts 90 minutes, for a group of up to 5 participants.
- Booking and payment are made in advance.
- An event can be cancelled up to 5 days before its date with no cancellation fee.
- A later cancellation is charged the full booking amount, except where the law grants a right to cancel or a refund.
- If the studio cancels the event, you receive a full refund.

## 9. Age of participants

- Teen classes are for ages 12 to 17.
- Regular classes are for participants aged 18 and over.
- Minors may join regular classes with the studio's approval, subject to a parent's or guardian's consent, the suitability of the class and the requirements of the law.
- Participation of minors requires the consent of a parent or guardian.

## 10. Health declaration

- Every participant must complete a digital health declaration in the app before their first class.
- Please let the studio know about any change in your health that may affect training.
- Where a medical clearance is required, it must be provided before participating.
- If you feel pain, dizziness or anything unusual, stop the activity and tell the instructor.

## 11. Studio conduct

- Come in suitable sportswear and non-slip grip socks.
- Keep the studio and the equipment clean.
- Switch mobile phones to silent.
- Use the equipment as the instructor directs.
- Respect the instructors and the other participants.

## 12. Safety and liability

- Physical exercise involves effort and the natural risks that come with training.
- You must share relevant health information and follow the instructor's guidance.
- The studio and its instructors must take reasonable precautions in accordance with the law.
- Nothing in these terms releases the studio or anyone acting on its behalf from liability under the law, including for negligence.

## 13. First aid

- The studio owner is trained in first aid.
- In the event of an injury or a medical incident, first response is given according to that training and the circumstances.
- Where needed, appropriate medical assistance is called.

## 14. Photography and publicity

- Participants are photographed for advertising and marketing only after separate, explicit consent.
- Consent to photography and publicity is not a condition of signing up or of taking part in classes.
- Consent to photography and publicity can be withdrawn by contacting the studio, subject to the law.
- Consent to photography and publicity is given on a separate form.

## 15. Privacy and personal data

- Personal and health information given through the app is used to run the studio's services and for the purposes set out in the privacy policy.
- The information is handled in accordance with the law and with appropriate security measures.
- The full privacy policy is presented separately.

## 16. Schedule changes

- The studio may change the schedule, class times and instructors as needed.
- If the studio cancels a class, the class does not count as used.
- Notice of changes is given as early as possible.

## 17. Cancelling a membership or a purchase

- A request to cancel a membership or a purchase can be made through the re:set pilates app.
- You can also contact us on WhatsApp or by phone at {phone}, or by email at {email}.
- A cancellation notice can also be given in the other ways set out in law, including by registered mail to the business address.
- The date the request is received is the date the cancellation notice is delivered.
- The reason for cancelling may be given as feedback only and is not a condition of cancelling.
- Stopping charges, refunds and cancellation fees, where permitted, are handled in accordance with the law.
- Cancelling a class booking does not automatically cancel the membership or the pass.

## 18. Accepting the terms

- The terms are accepted digitally through the re:set pilates app.
- Accepting the terms constitutes agreement to them, subject to applicable law.
- The studio may update the terms from time to time, giving notice of material changes and preserving customers' rights.
